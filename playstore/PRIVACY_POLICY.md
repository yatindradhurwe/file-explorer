# Privacy Policy for File Explore
**Last Updated:** October 27, 2026  
**Developer:** yjtechnosoft  
**Contact:** support@yjtechnosoft.com  

Thank you for choosing **File Explore** ("the App"), developed and maintained by **yjtechnosoft**. We respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal data. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, storage, permissions, and security.

---

### 1. Data Collection & Usage
**File Explore does not collect, harvest, store, or sell any personal or identifying information.**
- **No Analytics / Telemetry:** We do not track your usage patterns, screens visited, or device analytics.
- **No Third-Party Advertising:** The App does not contain third-party ad networks or tracking SDKs.
- **No Cloud Uploads:** Your files, media, contacts, documents, and directories remain strictly on your device.

---

### 2. Device Permissions & Purpose
To function as a complete file manager and peer-to-peer file transfer utility, File Explore requests certain system permissions:

| Permission | Technical Name | Reason & Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **All Files Access** | `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | Core file manager functionality: allows you to browse, search, open, copy, move, rename, and delete files on your internal storage and SD card. |
| **Media Access** | `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO` | Enables categorization and viewing of your photos, music, and videos within category galleries. |
| **Local Network / Wi-Fi** | `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`, `ACCESS_WIFI_STATE`, `CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE`, `INTERNET` | Required exclusively for **QuickDrop** peer-to-peer discovery and direct socket file transfer over your local Wi-Fi or mobile hotspot. No data is sent to external remote servers. |

---

### 3. QuickDrop Peer-to-Peer Transfer
The **QuickDrop** feature allows direct device-to-device wireless file transfer:
- Device discovery uses local subnet multicast broadcasts.
- Files are transmitted directly between two devices over an encrypted/direct local HTTP/TCP socket.
- Neither file contents nor device IDs are ever routed through or stored on external servers or cloud infrastructure.

---

### 4. Children’s Privacy
File Explore does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The App complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international child privacy regulations.

---

### 5. Security of Your Information
Because all operations and file interactions take place locally on your device, your files remain secured by your Android operating system's sandbox and file permissions.
